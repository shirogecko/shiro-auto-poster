Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Shiro Auto Poster is a desktop application designed to help its owner manage and publish video content to supported social media platforms.

2. Information We Access

The application may access account information and authorization data necessary to connect to supported platforms, including YouTube and TikTok.

3. Authentication Data

Authentication tokens and credentials are stored locally on the user's computer. These credentials are used only to provide authorized application functionality.

4. Video and Content Data

Video files and metadata are processed locally before being submitted to the selected platform. Published content is handled according to the policies of that platform.

5. Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal information. Data is shared with third-party platforms only when necessary to provide requested functionality.

6. Data Security

Users are responsible for protecting their devices, local files, and authentication credentials. Users should never share their access tokens or client secrets.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored application data may remain on the user's computer until manually deleted. Users may revoke platform access through the relevant platform account settings.

8. Third-Party Services

YouTube and TikTok have their own privacy policies. Users should review those policies when connecting their accounts.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application functionality or data practices change.
